Privacy

Updated 2026-10-28

Data controller

Ibrahima NDIAYE, sole proprietor, see the legal notice. Contact: contact@getvty.com.

The getvty.com website

  • No cookies, no ads.
  • Traffic is measured with GoatCounter, with no cookies and no cross-site tracking: only aggregate figures are kept (page views, referrer, browser, country, clicks on Download). The counting script is loaded from GoatCounter's servers (gc.zgo.at), which keep no full IP address and no tracking identifier. Its servers are at Hetzner (Finland and Germany), and the service is run from Ireland.
  • Fonts are hosted on the site itself: no request to Google.
  • GitHub, which hosts the site and the downloads, may log visitors' IP addresses in its technical logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Buying Vty Pro

Payment is processed by Polar (Polar Software, Inc., Dover, Delaware, USA), which collects your e-mail, name, country, billing address and payment details. The publisher never sees your card number. It receives from Polar your e-mail, name, country and order details, to send your key, answer you and keep accounts. See Polar's privacy policy.

The Vty software

  • Vty runs on your computer. Your devices, commands and backups are never sent to the publisher.
  • Passwords are saved only if you allow it, in an encrypted vault. SSH key passphrases are never saved.
  • Pro license (VTY- key): at activation, then about once a day, Vty sends Polar your license key, the public organization identifier and your PC's name, to check the license is valid and count devices. Offline, Pro stays active for 30 days. Removing the license frees the device. Offline-signed keys (VTY1-), given out by hand, contact no server.
  • At startup, at most once a day, Vty reads getvty.com/version.json to see whether a newer version exists, without sending any data. It can be turned off in the License window. Nothing is downloaded or installed automatically.
  • Optional, off by default: the vulnerability lookup queries Cisco's security API (PSIRT openVuln) with your devices' IOS version, using your own Cisco credentials.

Retention

Order data is kept as long as needed for the sale, support and accounting duties (10 years for accounting records). Messages sent to the contact address are kept while we answer you. Polar keeps its own data under its policy, as long as needed and for its legal obligations.

Your rights

You may ask for access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to contact@getvty.com. You may also complain to the CNIL.

Service providers

Polar (payment, license keys), GitHub (hosting), OVH (domain name, e-mail), GoatCounter (statistics). Polar and GitHub may process data outside the European Union. Polar states it relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses; for GitHub, see its privacy statement. GoatCounter is hosted in the European Union, OVH in France.
