Nothing leaves your computer.

Vty runs entirely on your PC. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

  • No username or password written to disk: they stay in memory for the duration of the connection.
  • Only reachable from your own computer (127.0.0.1), never from the network.
  • SSH fingerprint checked: the connection is blocked if the switch's key changes.
  • Old SHA-1 and CBC algorithms only used as a last resort, and flagged.
  • License checked on your computer, with no Internet connection.
  • CSV exports protected against Excel formula injection.

Frequently asked questions

Which devices does Vty work with?

The terminal works with any device reachable over SSH, Telnet or a serial console. Backups, the audit, macros and the vulnerability check are built for Cisco IOS and IOS-XE.

Where are my passwords stored?

Nowhere. Vty asks for them when you connect and keeps them in memory only as long as needed, never on disk. For an SSH key, only its location is saved; the passphrase is asked again.

Is the free version really free?

Yes, with no time limit and no sign-up. It's limited to 25 saved devices; the fleet management features (backups, audit, multi-exec…) need a Pro license.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that normal?

Yes: the installer isn't signed yet. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. You can check the file with its SHA-256 fingerprint and the VirusTotal scan further down this page.

Do I need an Internet connection?

No. Vty works on an isolated network, license included. It only checks for a new version at startup (you can turn that off), and the Cisco vulnerability check only contacts Cisco when you run it.

How does the Pro license work?
